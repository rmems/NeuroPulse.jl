- Extended `docs/interop.md` + `docs/src/interop.md` (kept byte-identical)

- Facts verified from source, not the issue text:
  - Live baseline is silicon-bridge `examples/live_telemetry_loop.rs`: 16
    lanes, 500 ms tick (`TICK_MS`), lane `seq % 5` active (silicon-hdl#112
    lowest-nonzero-lane decode), `max(norm * STIMULUS_GAIN(2.5), 1/256)`,
    signed Q8.8 over UART. Lanes 5–15 = 0.
  - `LiveTelemetryEncoder` (encode.rs) is a separate spike modality on a
    1 ms model clock (`TIMESTEP_SECONDS`); refused for the shipped analog
    bank (`SpikeModalityMismatch`).
  - Shipped readout: 16×3 neuron-major `parameters_output_weights.mem`,
    `score_readout` accumulates in `f64` → 3-wide `spikenaut-output-row-v1`
    (comfort/temp/power). Formula is `pred = W' * spikes` over the stored
    image.
  - Spike types: `NeuroPulse.Attention` owns `SpikeEvent`/`SpikeTrain`/
    `TemporalBuffer` (vendored from TemporalFocus.jl, ADR 0002);
    SpikeStream.jl has its own parametric `SpikeEvent{T,I}(time, neuron_id)`
    and owns stream features/encodings.
